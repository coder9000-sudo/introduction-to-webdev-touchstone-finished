Our Story

This bakery was founded in 1992 by a man named John Sykes, he believed that grocery store bread was not adequate. Fast forward to today and we are among the best bakeries in the city. We believe in the principles of being environmentally friendly and thus source everything from local farmers. In addition, all of our products are handmade.
